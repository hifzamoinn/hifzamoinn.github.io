SAP DATA & ANALYTICS / SOLUTION APPROACH

Enterprise Analytics & Reporting

A structured approach to business reporting with SAP Analytics Cloud, built on trusted data semantics.

REFERENCE ARCHITECTURE
Business requirements→Data model→SAC story→KPI validation→Business decisions
Illustrative technical approach. Specific implementations, configurations, and outcomes require project-level validation.

01. Understand business decisions

Define audiences, KPI definitions, drill paths, and business questions before building visualizations.

02. Prepare reporting data

Connect SAC to appropriate data sources and validate grains, filters, calculations, and aggregation rules.

03. Design interactive stories

Create readable layouts, clear trend and comparison views, and filters that serve practical business workflows.

04. Perform user validation

Reconcile metrics with source systems and collaborate with stakeholders on UAT, performance, and adoption.
