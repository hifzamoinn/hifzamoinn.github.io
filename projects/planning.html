BUSINESS OUTCOME

From distributor demand to controlled replenishment.

Primary sales establish sell-in and supply history. Secondary sales provide the downstream consumption signal. Inventory and confirmed inbound supply help determine replenishment timing and volume.

  • Automated suggestions and planner overrides
  • Allocation constraints and scenario comparison
  • Review and approval before release
  • Execution handoff to warehouse or ERP
  • Delivery feedback into the next planning cycle
SAC plans and governs allocations. ERP/WMS executes approved fulfillment.

03 / PLANNING ENGINEERING

Two allocation paths. One governed plan.

01 / AUTOMATION

Scheduled replenishment run

Data actions calculate proposed quantities and priority flags from projected demand, available inventory and confirmed pipeline. The result remains a proposal until approved.

02 / PLANNER CONTROL

Custom allocation form

Input-enabled planning interface supports distributor- and SKU-level adjustments, reasoned overrides, scenario versions and controlled recalculation.

03 / APPROVAL

Review to warehouse release

Calendar-based approval coordinates submissions, reviewers and released plans. Approved records are passed to fulfillment processes through a defined integration.

04 / MODEL & BUSINESS LOGIC

Deciding when and how much to replenish.

Illustrative replenishment logic

Projected Stock = On-hand + Confirmed Inbound − Forecast Consumption

Target Stock = Demand for Target Cover + Safety Stock

Net Replenishment = MAX(0, Target Stock − Projected Stock)

Released Qty = MIN(Net Replenishment, Allocatable Supply)

Pack sizes, lead time, minimum order quantities, blocked stock and delivery dates can introduce additional constraints.

Development components

  • Planning models with version, time, distributor and material dimensions
  • Data actions and advanced formulas for stock and coverage logic
  • Input-ready allocation tables and parameter-driven actions
  • Calendar tasks for review and approval
  • Data integration for primary / secondary actuals and inventory
  • Separate downstream execution and dispatch confirmation
