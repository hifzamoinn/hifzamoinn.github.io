SAP DATA & ANALYTICS / SOLUTION APPROACH

SAP Analytics Cloud Planning

A solution pattern for planning, allocation, and review processes using SAP Analytics Cloud.

REFERENCE ARCHITECTURE
Actuals and drivers→SAC planning model→Data actions→Review and approval→Operational handoff
Illustrative technical approach. Specific implementations, configurations, and outcomes require project-level validation.

01. Establish the planning foundation

Combine validated actuals with planning dimensions, versions, time, and measures in an SAC planning model.

02. Model allocations and scenarios

Use data actions and advanced formulas, where appropriate, to automate calculations, allocations, and what-if planning.

03. Design the planning experience

Provide controlled input tables and planning interfaces with clear assumptions, ownership, and validation.

04. Coordinate approval and execution

Implement review and approval through an appropriate planning or external workflow design, with approved allocations passed to operational execution systems through an integration layer.
