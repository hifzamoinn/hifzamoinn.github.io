SAP DATA & ANALYTICS / SOLUTION APPROACH

SAP Datasphere Engineering

An architectural overview of how SAP Datasphere brings heterogeneous business data into reusable, governed models.

REFERENCE ARCHITECTURE
SAP / Non-SAP sources→Connections & ingestion→Datasphere spaces and modeling→Analytical model→SAC consumption
Illustrative technical approach. Specific implementations, configurations, and outcomes require project-level validation.

01. Connect the landscape

Integrate SAP and non-SAP sources using connectivity and ingestion patterns appropriate to source capabilities, latency, and operational constraints.

02. Transform and model

Structure source data in layers, apply business transformations and harmonized dimensions, and expose reusable consumption models.

03. Deliver semantic consistency

Use dimensions, facts, measures, relationships, and analytical models to establish definitions that business teams can trust.

04. Validate and operate

Reconcile results with source records, assess refresh and performance needs, and maintain lineage and access controls.
