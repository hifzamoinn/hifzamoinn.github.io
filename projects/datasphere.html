INTEGRATION / SAP & NON-SAP

Experience across heterogeneous data sources.

My integration exposure covers enterprise applications, traditional databases, API-based services, and integration middleware, with different acquisition patterns selected according to each system’s interfaces and requirements.

01 / SAP APPLICATIONS

S/4HANA, ECC & CDS

Integrating operational SAP data using eligible CDS extraction views, source structures, remote tables and replication approaches; managing keys, business filters and extraction dependencies.

02 / PROCUREMENT

SAP Ariba

Bringing procurement and supplier data into analytical models through available APIs and staged integrations, then reconciling supplier identities, project statuses, approvals and lifecycle measures.

03 / HUMAN RESOURCES

SAP SuccessFactors

Working with HR-related source entities and supported remote-table or data-flow patterns, and integrating workforce dimensions into cross-functional reporting.

04 / THIRD-PARTY DATABASES

SQL Server & MySQL

Connecting relational sources to Datasphere, developing extraction and mapping logic, handling datatype compatibility, and building harmonized models from non-SAP operational records.

05 / API-LED INTEGRATION

OData & REST Services

Working across API-based data exchange, entity structures, pagination, mappings and ingestion intermediaries to turn service responses into structured analytical datasets.

06 / MIDDLEWARE

SAP CPI / Integration Suite

Integrating CPI-pushed or middleware-delivered data into staging tables and Open SQL schemas, then transforming and incorporating it into Datasphere business models.

ENGINEERING / HANDS-ON CAPABILITIES

Development beyond connectivity.

DATA TRANSFORMATION

SQL & graphical development

Designing reusable views and transformations with joins, filters, unions, aggregations, case expressions, calculated columns, datatype conversions and source-to-target mappings. Managing grain and keys to avoid duplication and inaccurate measures.

WAREHOUSING

Layered data architecture

Structuring data into foundational, composite and consumption-ready layers; organizing source-aligned tables, reusable master data, operational facts and cross-domain business datasets.

SEMANTIC DESIGN

Metrics business users can trust

Creating facts and dimensions, associations, texts, hierarchies, calculated measures, time-based logic and consistent KPI definitions through Datasphere analytical models.

INTEGRATION ENGINEERING

Replications & API bridges

Working with initial loads and supported delta mechanisms, ingestion schedules, source extraction constraints, middleware handoffs, schema changes, compatibility issues and integration troubleshooting.

DATA GOVERNANCE

Security & controlled access

Applying space-based organization, cross-space data sharing, model exposure controls, data access controls and clear separation between reusable enterprise models and reporting-specific datasets.

QUALITY & OPERATIONS

Validation & performance

Reconciling source-to-target totals, distinct counts, missing records and mapping discrepancies; examining refresh status, query performance, record-level logic and consumption consistency.

DELIVERY / QUALITY ASSURANCE

Reliable models need reliable controls.

Source-to-target reconciliationValidate record counts, unique business keys, missing entities and source coverage before exposing datasets.
Complex business-rule translationConvert operational conditions, date logic, processing statuses and workflow states into understandable metrics.
